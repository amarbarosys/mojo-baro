Bugs found on the way, both mine, both arena lifetimes written by hand: `xn` was planned dead after the attention
norm (the gate GEMV then overwrote its own input), then dead before the final norm (the lm head overwrote its input).
Found by comparing per-layer tensor sums against `llama-eval-callback` (layer 0: every op within ~1%; l_out all 16
layers within ~1%).

Prefill (`kernels/llama/llama.mojo` (forward over TP rows)): weights dequantised per GEMM into a 32 MB f16 scratch with the decode GEMV's own
unpack (kq_lane16), then the WMMA GEMM; attention is mojo-baro's GQA decode kernel, which is causal over rows. mojo-baro's
amar_attn_prefill assumes 256-wide heads (32 lanes x 8) and wrote out of row at HD 64 (run-to-run noise in layer 1's KV).
Dequant per element (q4k_value) cost ~15 ms of 38; the 16-wide unpack brought 511 tokens from 38.4 to 23.4 ms.
